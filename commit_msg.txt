Phase 5 (SMBH-mass scan, design complete, not yet run): does the threshold picture
generalize to SMBH masses other than the Milky Way's? Design decided with the user: hold
the cluster's structural profile fixed (N26 never specifies how it would scale with
m_smbh) and vary only m_smbh across 7 log-spaced points (1.26e5-1.26e8 Msun, centered on
N26's own 4e6 anchor), H18 IC, both Eq. 22 readings, 8 seeds/point from the start.

Two real problems found by smoke-testing before committing any real compute, not
by reasoning alone -- exactly this project's established practice:
1. population.A_MIN_PC_DEFAULT (the inner semimajor-axis sampling bound) was tuned
   specifically for m_smbh=4e6; naively generalizing it (new
   population.a_min_safety_bound helper) let it shrink at low m_smbh, which samples into
   a region where the held-fixed density profile blows up to ~9000x its calibration value,
   collapsing collision timescales and driving the step count toward its ceiling (a
   smoke-test hang, >40 min, not a hypothetical). Fixed by clamping: a_min only grows
   beyond the default (needed at high m_smbh), never shrinks below it.
2. Even after that fix, low-m_smbh + star_only runs can still be dramatically slow --
   likely genuine runaway growth becoming more severe at low m_smbh (lower velocity
   dispersion boosts collision efficiency everywhere; lower coulomb_log weakens the
   moderating relaxation process). One seed at m_smbh=4e5 did not converge within 15
   minutes. Decided with the user: run the full grid as designed anyway and track
   whether each run hits the 2,000,000-step ceiling (hit_step_ceiling column) as data in
   its own right, rather than narrowing the grid or discarding slow runs.
