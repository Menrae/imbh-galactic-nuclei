Add IMBH galactic-nuclei reimplementation: Phases 0-2 complete, Phase 3 in progress

Reimplements and extends the semianalytic dynamical-formation model from Newton
et al. 2026 (ApJ, 1006:184), which asks whether intermediate-mass black holes
can form in nuclear star clusters through ordinary stellar collisions and BH-BH
GW-capture mergers.

Phase 0 - Scaffolding
- src/tests/docs/paper/figures/results/scripts/config layout, pyproject.toml
- SimulationConfig dataclass system (cluster/population/integration), YAML
  load/save, config/default.yaml matching the paper's fiducial run

Phase 1 - Core physics (Sections 2 and 4 of the paper)
- cluster.py, gw_capture.py, recoil.py, collisions.py, relaxation.py,
  inspiral.py: every equation implemented and unit-tested against the primary
  source PDF (read directly, not paraphrased) plus five further papers pulled
  in to resolve equations the paper cites but doesn't fully specify
  (Holley-Bockelmann 2008, Volonteri 2013, Rose 2020, Rose 2022,
  Barausse & Rezzolla 2009)
- Caught and corrected a real error versus an earlier paraphrased pass
  (velocity dispersion was missing a (alpha+1) factor)
- Resolved a genuinely undefined term in the paper's final-spin equation by
  tracing it to Barausse & Rezzolla 2009, verified against their own published
  NR calibration benchmark to 3 decimal places
- Flagged, with reasoning, a likely typo in the paper's spin-change equation
  (exponent mismatch against its own cited source)
- docs/equations.md and paper/limitations.md maintained throughout as the
  living record of every equation's sourcing and every judgment call

Phase 2 - Monte Carlo integration loop
- orbital_dynamics.py (Kepler mechanics, velocity-kick application),
  population.py (initial orbital sampling), simulation.py (the full
  per-timestep probabilistic update loop with structured DataFrame output)
- Three real bugs found via full-loop stress testing (not visible in
  isolated unit tests) and fixed with regression tests: a negative
  GW-capture cross section at small radii, BHs permanently stuck mid-orbit
  after a kick, and an unphysical relaxation-kick blowup from an uncapped
  adaptive timestep

Phase 3 - Validation (in progress)
- initial_conditions.py: all four published initial BH mass/spin
  distributions (K20, K20+M, H18, H18+M), including a real pre-ship bug fix
  (decorrelated mass/spin draws for the merger-product "+M" variants)
- H18 confirmed as an exact closed form from Hoang et al. 2018; K20
  reconstructed from the paper's own Figure 1 histogram, since its cited
  source (Kremer et al. 2020) is a numerical simulation with no closed-form
  mass function to transcribe
- A real N=1000, 10 Gyr validation run against Table 1 does not yet match:
  the EMRI rate is far above the paper's implied rate. Root cause narrowed
  to the relaxation-kick timestep aggregation, not the merger physics
  (independently verified against a numerical-relativity benchmark) --
  documented in detail in paper/limitations.md as the next concrete task

214 tests passing. 10 reference papers archived in references/.
