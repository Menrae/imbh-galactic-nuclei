Phase 4: critical-mass threshold scan, both Eq. 22 readings -- finds a gradual
crossover under star_only, no threshold under bh_inclusive

Scopes and runs the first pass of Phase 4 (N26's own stated open question: is
there a critical initial-mass-distribution threshold for IMBH formation, and
is any such transition sharp or gradual?). The one hard requirement carried
over from Phase 3's still-open Eq. 22 <M_avg>/rho ambiguity: every conclusion
had to be checked against both readings, not computed once under the
star-only default.

Config refactor (prerequisite, not new physics)
- Added IntegrationConfig.relaxation_mass_weighting: Literal["star_only",
  "bh_inclusive"], replacing the hardcoded star-only implementation in
  simulation._timescales and a new shared helper _relaxation_mass_and_density
  (also used by _local_t_relax for the substepped relaxation walk). Both code
  paths already existed and were already empirically tested during the Phase 3
  investigation (paper/limitations.md#average-object-mass) -- this makes
  switching between them a config choice instead of an inline edit.
- Does not touch Eq. 23's segregation_timescale, which N26 states as star-only
  regardless of this field.

Mass-distribution scan family
- initial_conditions.sample_log_uniform_mass/log_uniform_mean/
  get_log_uniform_samplers: a log-uniform family on [6, m_max] Msun,
  generalizing H18's own functional form (H18 is the m_max=100 endpoint
  exactly). Scan parameterization and every alternative considered (a K20/H18
  mixture blend, scanning the primordial-binary fraction too) proposed to and
  confirmed by the user before running anything, per
  paper/limitations.md#phase4-mass-family-scan.
- log_uniform_mean has an exact closed form (unlike K20's reconstructed
  sampler), confirmed to match H18's independently Monte-Carlo-measured mean.

Pass 1: scripts/phase4_mass_threshold_scan.py
- 9 m_max grid points (16-100 Msun) x both Eq. 22 readings x 3 seeds = 54
  runs, N=1000, 10 Gyr, primordial fraction 0.
- Headline: the threshold's existence is NOT robust to the Eq. 22 ambiguity.
  star_only rises smoothly from 0% to 12.7% (BHs > 100 Msun) across the grid;
  bh_inclusive stays at an exact, flat 0.0% for m_max <= 79.5 and only reaches
  a marginal 0.1-0.3% at m_max=100 itself.
- This falsifies a working assumption from Phase 3 (that bulk-population
  statistics would be far less sensitive to this ambiguity than the extreme
  mass tail) -- "% BHs > 100 Msun" is itself a bulk statistic, and it's about
  as sensitive to this choice as anything in the whole investigation.
- Pipeline validation: the m_max=100/star_only grid point is equivalent by
  construction to a Phase 3 H18 run and reproduces it bit-for-bit in 2 of 3
  seeds; the third seed's small divergence is fully explained (a more precise
  closed-form mean_bh_mass shifted one chaotic trajectory's timestep sizing).

Pass 2: scripts/phase4b_threshold_refinement.py
- Pass 1's apparent "critical band" (25-32 Msun) rested on a single seed
  flipping 1-of-3 at one grid point -- not enough to state a location. Ran 31
  more star_only-only seeds (8 total per point instead of 3) at 5 grid points
  spanning the band. bh_inclusive did not need this: its finding was already
  saturated at 24/24 runs reading an exact 0.0%.
- Result: the refinement overturned the pass-1 read, not just tightened it.
  m_max=31.8, which looked fully saturated at 3/3, turned out to be 6/8 (75%,
  Wilson 95% CI 41-93%) once properly sampled. The "any IMBH forms"
  probability rises smoothly and without saturating across the whole band:
  0% (20.1) -> 38% (22.6) -> 50% (25.3) -> 75% (28.4, 31.8).
- This is a direct, evidenced answer to the sharper of N26's own two framings
  of the open question: under star_only, the transition is gradual, not
  sharp, and hasn't even saturated by 31.8 Msun -- well under a third of the
  way to H18's own 100 Msun ceiling.

paper/methodology.md (new)
- A 9-gate checklist for when a secondary finding graduates from "preliminary
  observation" to "reportable result": primary-source grounding (three-way
  classification of what N26 states / is ambiguous about / is purely our own
  choice), noise-floor comparison, adequate sample size for the specific claim
  being made, mechanism (not just pattern), a falsification/adversarial pass,
  an internal consistency cross-check against prior validated results, an
  explicit scope statement, framing discipline specifically for findings that
  touch N26's own text (never imply error without checking the original cited
  source; report every textually-defensible reading, not the cleanest one),
  and a reproducibility artifact.
- Built from practices this project was already using ad hoc (the Eq. 22
  investigation), not invented from scratch. Applied as a worked scorecard
  against the Phase 4 threshold finding: every gate passed after pass 1 except
  sample size for the location claim specifically, which is exactly what pass
  2 was run to close -- and closing it changed the finding, which is the
  scorecard's own demonstration of why the gate exists.
